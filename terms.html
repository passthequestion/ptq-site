Terms of Service

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. What this covers

These terms cover the "Pass the Question" posting tool (the "Tool"). The Tool is a private script that uploads videos made by the Pass the Question channel to the channel's own TikTok account as drafts, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

2. Who can use it

The Tool is used only by the owner of the Pass the Question channel, for the channel's own TikTok account. It is not a public service: no one else can sign up for it or connect their account to it.

3. What the Tool does

  • It asks TikTok for permission to read the account's basic profile (to confirm the right account is connected) and to upload videos as drafts.
  • It sends a video file to the account's TikTok inbox. Nothing is published by the Tool. The account owner opens the draft in the TikTok app, reviews it, and decides whether and when to post it.
  • It does not post comments, send messages, follow accounts, read other users' data, or change account settings.

4. Content

All videos sent with the Tool are created by the Pass the Question channel. The channel is responsible for its content and for following TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service, including labelling AI-generated content.

5. TikTok's rules

Use of TikTok through the Tool is also subject to TikTok's own terms and developer policies. If TikTok changes or removes access, the Tool may stop working.

6. No warranty

The Tool is provided as is, for the channel's own use, without any warranty.
