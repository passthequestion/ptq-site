Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

1. Summary

The Pass the Question posting tool (the "Tool") is used only by the channel owner for the channel's own TikTok account. It collects the minimum TikTok gives it to work, keeps it on the owner's own computer, and shares it with no one.

2. What data the Tool receives from TikTok

  • Access and refresh tokens — so the Tool can upload drafts to the connected account.
  • Basic profile information (account ID and display name) — only to confirm the correct account is connected.

The Tool does not collect followers, viewers, comments, messages, location, contacts or any data about other TikTok users.

3. How it is used and stored

  • Tokens are stored in a private file on the channel owner's computer, readable only by that user account. They are not uploaded to any server, database or website.
  • Data is used only to upload the channel's own videos as drafts and to check the upload status.
  • No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

4. Videos

The videos sent with the Tool are made by the channel. They are uploaded directly to TikTok, and TikTok handles them under its own privacy policy.

5. How long it is kept, and deleting it

Tokens are kept until they expire or the owner disconnects the Tool. To remove access at any time, remove the app's permission in the TikTok app's settings and delete the local token file.

6. Children

The Tool is not directed at children and is not used with anyone else's account.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: thequestionpass@gmail.com.
